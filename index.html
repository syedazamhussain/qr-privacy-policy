Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to the QR Scanner & Generator mobile application. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting it through our compliance with this policy.

1. Information We Do Not Collect

We do not collect, store, or sell any personally identifiable information (PII) such as your name, email address, phone number, or physical location. Any text, URLs, or data you input to generate QR codes remain strictly on your device.

2. Permissions Used by the App

  • Camera Permission: Required solely to scan QR codes in real-time. Video streams from the camera are processed live on your device and are never recorded, saved, or uploaded to any remote server.
  • Storage / Photos Permission: Used only when you choose to save a generated QR code image to your device's gallery. The app does not access any other private photos or files.

3. Third-Party Services and Advertisements

The app may display advertisements served by Google AdMob. These third-party ad networks may collect and use pseudonymous device identifiers (such as Android Advertising ID), cookies, and diagnostic data to serve relevant ads according to their privacy policies.

For more details on how Google uses information from apps, visit: Google Privacy & Terms.

4. Data Security

All processing related to QR scanning and creation happens entirely locally on your device. We implement industry-standard safeguards to ensure your usage remains private.

5. Children's Privacy

Our app does not knowingly collect personal data from children under the age of 13. If you believe your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can take appropriate action.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.
