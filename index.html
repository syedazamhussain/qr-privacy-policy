Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to the QR Scanner & Generator mobile application. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting it through our compliance with this policy.

1. Information We Do Not Collect

We do not collect, store, or sell any personally identifiable information (PII) such as your name, email address, phone number, or physical location. Any text, URLs, or data you scan or input to generate QR codes remain strictly on your local device.

2. Permissions Used by the App

  • Camera Permission: Required solely to scan QR codes and barcodes in real-time. Video streams from the camera are processed live on your device and are never recorded, saved, or uploaded to any remote server.
  • Storage / Media Permission: Used only when you choose to save a generated QR code image to your device's gallery or scan an image from local storage. The app does not access any other unrelated files or photos.

3. Third-Party Services and In-App Purchases

The app integrates trusted third-party services that may collect information used to identify your device:

  • Google AdMob: To display personalized or non-personalized advertisements. AdMob may collect and process pseudonymous identifiers (such as Android Advertising ID), IP address, and app diagnostics. For details, visit: Google Ads Privacy & Terms.
  • Google Play In-App Billing: Used to handle optional one-time purchases (such as Ad-Free upgrades). All payment processing and financial data are securely managed directly by Google Play. We do not collect, process, or store your credit/debit card numbers or payment credentials. For details, visit: Google Privacy Policy.

4. Data Security

All scanning, decoding, and QR code generation occurs entirely offline on your device. We implement industry-standard practices to ensure your usage remains completely private and secure.

5. Children's Privacy

Our app does not knowingly collect personal data from children under the age of 13. If you believe your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can take appropriate action.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.
